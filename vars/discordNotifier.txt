Queues the current Jenkins build result for delivery to Discord. The worker
holds the webhook credential and retries messages that Discord has not
confirmed.

workerJob selects the release-notifier worker. The optional projectName is used
in the footer. repository or githubUrl enables commit links. version and
releaseLinks can be supplied explicitly.

For Gradle mod projects, the step can read githubUrl, specificationVersion,
curseHomepageUrl, and modrinthId from gradle.properties. It also reads
CurseForge and Modrinth links written by the Mod Publish Plugin.

getGradleReleaseMetadata() returns projectName, version, minecraftVersion,
modLoaders, and releaseLinks for passing to releaseNotifier.
